## 1. Eight cast-list entries were missing

The first version asked only about **speech prefixes**. Eight questions concern
entries in the **cast list** itself, and those were dropped. They are below.

They need a different question from a speech prefix. The usual problem is not
that we cannot tell who it is, but that the tagged span is wrong: Meshumed's
`א` covers only the article of `א ריכטיר פֿון געהיימס געריכט`, a role the cast
list already contains. So these entries offer *the span is mis-cut* and *one
span, several roles* as answers, rather than making you choose between names.
