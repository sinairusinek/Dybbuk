Prepared 2026-09-30 for the meeting of Sunday 4 October 2026.
Context: Education is the first type taken to 100%, so the choices made in it become the working precedent for the remaining 4,047 rows. These questions are about making that precedent explicit.

## Q1. Is the deferral rule "generic institution, no linking value"?

Our reading of the 559 deferrals is that they are mostly cases where the name does not individuate an institution — `אָרטיקער קאָנסערוואַטאָריע` ("the local conservatory"), bare `רעאַל-שול`, `שטאָטישע שול` — so minting would assert a shared institution where the sources only support "both were in the same city."

The strongest evidence for this reading: **92% of the deferred rows are singleton clusters** (one mention, one person), and the multi-mention ones are largely generic kinds attested across unrelated cities:

- `בית-המדרש` — 9 mentions across Vienna, Solechnik, Pagevezh
- `פּאָליטעכניקום` — 4 across Mexico City, Nancy, Karlsruhe
- `יידישע שול` — 6 across Bucharest and Bergen-Belsen

**Is that the rule you were applying?** If so we would like to write it down as project policy, because it should govern the other 20 unfinished types too.

## Q2. Then why were 515 Education orgs minted, 77% of them singletons?

This is the question we could not answer from the data, and it is the main thing we need from you.

Minted rows include bare `קאָנסערוואַטאָריע` at cluster size 1, and `אַן אייגענע שולע` ("a school of his own") — which look like exactly the generic, non-individuating cases that were deferred elsewhere.

**22 identical names received both NEW and DEFER decisions:**

| Name | NEW | DEFER | ALIGN |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| קאָנסערוואַטאָריע | 6 | 4 | 0 |
| רעאַל-שול | 2 | 8 | 0 |
| אוניווערזיטעט | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| אָרטיקער קאָנסערוואַטאָריע | 2 | 3 | 0 |
| האַנדלס-שול | 1 | 3 | 0 |
| רוסישע גימנאַזיע | 1 | 3 | 0 |
| שטאָטישע שול | 2 | 2 | 0 |

Presumably the distinguishing evidence is in the source passage rather than the cluster name — e.g. the mention names the city, or the biography makes the institution identifiable. **What was the deciding factor?** If it is "the source passage individuates it even when the cluster label does not," that is a workable rule, but it means the cluster name alone is never sufficient grounds, and we should say so in the guidelines.

## Q4. What is the rule for `DEFER` vs `DESCRIPTIVE`?

Education has 559 DEFER and 51 DESCRIPTIVE, and from outside the distinction is not obvious. If DESCRIPTIVE means "this phrase never denotes an institution" and DEFER means "it might, but we cannot resolve which," then a large share of the deferrals — `אָרטיקער קאָנסערוואַטאָריע`, `אַן אייגענע שולע` — may belong under DESCRIPTIVE, which would close them permanently instead of leaving them queued.

## Q5. Does the rule generalise to the other types?

83% of all remaining undecided rows (3,375 of 4,047) are singletons, so whatever we decide here largely determines the size of the remaining job:

- **Theatre** — 1,394 singletons of 1,710 undecided
- **Journals/Newspapers** — 385 of 510
- **Publisher** — 267 of 305
- **Jewish political bodies** — 214 of 261

A `שטאָטישער טעאַטער` mentioned once is the same problem as a `שטאָטישע שול` mentioned once. **Should the Education rule be applied as project policy across all types?**
