## 2026-11-08 (Sinai)

- **A1 — general rule for translated plays: accepted.** A production counts
  as the L/H version only when the sentence names Lateiner or Hurwitz, or it
  is their own troupe or theatre when their version was in the repertoire.
  Otherwise it is a production of the original, translator unknown.
- **A2 — ווילהעלם טעל is Lateiner's** (Joseph and Izela's 1896 translation),
  not Hurwitz's. PL-0174 re-ascribed; Hershele's 1929 translation (5 facts)
  excluded.
- **A4 — עתליהו: agreed.** All productions are Hurwitz's; ascription certain.
- **A5 — מאָנטע קריסטאָ: agreed.** Hurwitz's 1894 version kept; Yarikhovsky's
  1889 music and the unplaced Dina Feinman photo excluded.
- **A7 — קיען / ליבע און ליידנשאַפט: agreed.** The two Shor plays and the 1916
  Landau debut excluded.
- **P1 — confirmed.** The authorship rebuild and the curators' verdicts
  stand. The 1911 „דאָס יודישע קינד" printing is a false ascription that we
  chose to document rather than drop: `YiDraCor/data/editions.csv`
  author_certainty = false ascription (Lateiner's lexicon entry: written by
  Shomer-Shaykevitsh).
- **P3 — rule:** an unvetted ascription counts as certain when the catalogue
  does not mark it otherwise and the lexicon attests it. Applied to 15
  Hurwitz plays found in Hurwitz's lexicon entry: PL-0041, 0051, 0063, 0083,
  0138, 0155, 0161, 0175, 0184, 0190, 0215, 0217, 0233, 0240, 0253 (four of
  them, PL-0041/0051/0063/0083, had Lateiner as author from the corrupt
  export). The eight the lexicon does not attest went back to Ruthie with
  their sources (P3 in `REVIEW_TASKS.md`).
- **R1 — yes.** „מאַלווינאַ לאָבעלס ראָיאָל טעאַטער" = מאָלווינע לאָבעלס
  ראָיאַל-טעאַטער (core_db 2250); aligned in `kg_link_review.tsv`. Note: core_db
  also has **2909 „לאָבעלס ראָיאַל-טעאַטער"** for the same New York theatre — a
  duplicate to merge in Zalmen.
