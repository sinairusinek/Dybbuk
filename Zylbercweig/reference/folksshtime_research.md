# Lodzsher Folksshtime: a pre-war paper, not the 1945 one

Sinai, 2026-10-10: "you write that Galron's run starts 1945 — but is it the
newspaper's history or just the available digitized copies?"

**The digitised copies.** And the question catches a real error on my part: the
README of this very directory states that Galron's years are digitisation
coverage and must never be used to date a paper's run. I wrote them up as the
paper's history anyway, and concluded from it.

## What Galron actually says

`galron-0826` · פֿאָלקס־שטימע · לודז׳–ווארשה (פולין) · **1945–1991** ·
Academica · <https://academica.edu.pl/reading/readStruct?uid=37406701>

That is the Academica holding, nothing more.

## The 1945/46 paper

*Folks-shtime* / Fołks-Sztyme, founded in **Łódź**, first issue **22 February
1946** per Polish Wikipedia and POLIN/sztetl (YIVO is quoted as April 1945,
which probably conflates it with *Dos naye lebn*, Łódź 1945–1950, a distinct
Central Committee daily it effectively succeeded). Organ of the PPR/PZPR, from
1950–56 of the TSKŻ. Moved to **Warsaw in 1949**. Ran to 1991; succeeded by
*Słowo Żydowskie / Dos Jidisze Wort*. Wikidata Q838352 treats it as one entity
with inception 1946 and names no predecessor; English Wikipedia asserts in
passing that "an homonymous newspaper existed before World War II" but gives no
place or date.

## There WAS a pre-war Lodz Folksshtime

From the biography of **Yisroel-Avrom Kahan** (1888–1943) in the *Leksikon fun
der nayer yidisher literatur*, verified in the page text:

> Over the years 1916-1920, he led the editorial board of his brother **Lazar
> Kahan's** *Lodzer folksblat* … **and a longer time with an evening newspaper
> *Lodzer folksshtime* (Lodz people's voice).**

<http://yleksikon.blogspot.com/2019/01/yisroel-avrom-kahan.html>

So a *Lodzer folksshtime* existed as an **evening paper around 1916–1920** —
squarely inside the Zylbercweig lexicon's window. Plausibly the evening
companion to Lazar Kahan's *Lodzer folksblat* (founded March 1915 under German
occupation), which would explain its absence from title-level bibliographies.

**Confidence: medium.** One source family (the Leksikon, echoed by Museum of
Family History). It is absent from the JewishGen Lodz periodicals inventory,
the baedekerlodz survey of the Lodz Jewish press, Polona and Wikidata, and no
independent issue run or years were found beyond Kahan's involvement.

## Decision

`ORG-C03416` *Lodzsher Folksshtime* → **REJECT** the link to `galron-0826`.
They are different papers: an evening paper of c. 1916–1920 against the
post-Holocaust party organ of 1946–1991.

Note what changed and what did not. My reasoning was wrong — the dates do not
show "the run starts 1945" — but the decision is the same, now resting on
positive evidence of an earlier paper rather than on a misreading of a
coverage range.

## Carry forward

Galron's `years_covered` is **digitisation coverage**. It may not be used to
date a paper, to reject a match, or to argue that a paper did not exist before
a given year. The matcher already treats it as advisory (`span_check`); prose
and research notes must do the same.
