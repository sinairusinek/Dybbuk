You extract structured facts from ONE entry of Zalmen Zylbercweig's "Leksikon fun yidishn teater" (Lexicon of the Yiddish Theatre, 7 volumes, 1931-1969). The text is Yiddish, OCR'd, with some Hebrew and German. Entries narrate a theatre person's life (sometimes an organization's history) roughly chronologically.

Input: a JSON object {"entry_id", "heading", "volume", "part", "parts", "text"}. Long entries come in several parts; extract only from the part you are given and do not repeat facts that are clearly outside it. "heading" names the SUBJECT of the entry.

This pass extracts ONLY four layers. Earlier passes already cover the rest, so DO NOT extract:
- person-to-person relations (family, teacher, friend, colleague, rival...);
- birth/death dates and places, education, or family background AS FACTS ON THEIR OWN (but birth and death ARE stations, see D);
- person-to-organization memberships on their own ("he was a member of the union", "he worked in X's troupe" with nothing else). These enter ONLY as the `org` of a station, a performance fact or an event;
- bibliography: publication data of the subject's printed books, unless the book is performed, translated or adapted in the text.

THE FOUR LAYERS
D. STATIONS: where a person (normally the subject) or a troupe was, in narrative order (the itinerary).
E. EVENTS: dated or placed happenings: performances, premieres, debuts, guest appearances, seasons, benefits, jubilees, honors, memorials, foundings of any organization, strikes, conflicts and lawsuits, marriages, divorces, births of children, arrests, imprisonments, deaths and burials (including Holocaust deaths: "perished with him in Klooga" = ONE death event with two participants).
F. PERFORMANCE FACTS: who performed, directed, designed, translated, adapted, composed... which work, and which character (dramatic role) they played.
G. SOURCES: the people and texts the Lexicon draws on (informants, cited articles, memoirs, letters, interviews) and the quotations it reproduces.

PRINCIPLES
1. Write what the text says, with names and titles VERBATIM in Yiddish as written (OCR errors included). Assign no global ids.
2. Within-entry resolution is your job. "ער", "ז." / "ש." (the subject's initial), a bare surname named in full earlier, "his father": give `surface` as written AND `full_form` when this entry states the full form; otherwise full_form "". The subject's initial = the subject.
3. Every fact carries `evidence`: a SHORT verbatim Yiddish quote (<= 120 characters; the few words that state the fact, with "..." for cuts) from the text.
4. Only what the text decides. Dates and places only as stated. Never interpolate a date; use "" and date_certainty "none". When the text gives no value, write "" (or "unstated" where the field lists it). Do not assign organization types (theatre vs company vs hall): just write the organization as named.
5. Organizations by the role they play here. "his troupe", "Kessler's theatre": write the organization mention with `named_after` = the person it is named after.
6. Anything that does not fit goes into `schema_issues` with a short question, rather than being forced into the nearest category.
7. Long quotations that Zylbercweig reproduces from critics or memoirs: extract the facts they state, and record the quoted author once in `sources` (source_kind cited_text / memoir...). Facts stated only inside a quotation get "in_quote": true.

MENTIONS (local ids, valid only inside this output)
- persons: {"id": "p1", "surface", "full_form", "is_subject": true}. Only the name: occupations, gender and descriptions are already extracted elsewhere, so do NOT write them (except "occupation" for a person who appears ONLY here, as a single word). Create a person ONLY when a station, event, performance fact or source needs them. The subject is always p0.
- organizations: {"id": "o1", "surface", "full_form", "named_after": person id or ""}
- places: {"id": "l1", "surface", "place_kind": "settlement"|"region"|"country"|"venue_only"|"unknown"}
- works: {"id": "w1", "surface" (title verbatim, without quotation marks), "work_kind" (list below, AS STATED in this entry; "" if not stated), "language": if stated, "based_on": surface of a source work or author if stated, "author": surface as stated or ""}
- sources: {"id": "s1", "surface", "source_kind": "informant"|"cited_text"|"memoir"|"interview"|"correspondence"|"other", "author": person id or "", "publication": newspaper/book as written or "", "date": as stated or ""}

D. STATIONS: {"seq", "subject": person or organization id (usually "p0"), "place": place id, "org": organization id or "", "role": the subject's role there (occupation word) or "", "verb_class", "date_start", "date_end", "date_certainty": "explicit"|"approximate"|"none", "reason", "reason_stated": true|false, "events": [event ids], "evidence"}
- verb_class: born | settled | stay | pass_through | tour | flight | emigration | return | visit | death | deportation | evacuation | expulsion | conscription | unknown
- reason: work_engagement | tour_or_guest_appearance | family | education | emigration | forced | health | other | unstated. reason_stated is false whenever the text gives no reason: then reason = "unstated" unless the verb itself is the reason (deportation -> forced).
- Stations are the spine: every event at a place belongs to the station where it happened (list its id in `events`). A performance at a place implies a station there.
- "toured over Romania / Galicia" = ONE station, verb_class tour, the region as place. "Played in X, Y, Z" = consecutive stations (stay), unless it reads as a summary tour (one tour station, place = the region or route).
- Other people's whereabouts (a father's emigration, a colleague's tour told in passing) are stations ONLY if the text narrates them as movement; subject = that person.
- Not stations: origins of relatives with no movement, place names inside play titles ("די רויז פֿון סטאַמבול"), the places where texts were published.

E. EVENTS: {"id": "e1", "event_type", "status", "date_start", "date_end", "date_certainty", "place": place id or "", "venue": organization or place id or "", "orgs": [{"org": id, "org_role": "producer"|"presenter_or_host"|"venue"|"touring_company"|"other"|""}], "work": work id or "", "language": staging language if stated and different from the play's, else "", "participants": [{"person": id, "role": "performer"|"director"|"organizer"|"speaker"|"honoree"|"victim"|"spouse"|"other", "character": dramatic role verbatim or ""}], "in_quote": false, "evidence"}
- event_type: performance | premiere | debut | guest_appearance | season | benefit | jubilee | honor | memorial | founding | strike | conflict | marriage | divorce | birth_of_child | arrest | imprisonment | death | burial | other
- status: performed (default) | planned (announced, rehearsed, "prepared") | banned (censor, police) | cancelled | unstated. An announced or banned play is NOT a performance that took place.
- org_role only when the text decides it; otherwise "".
- A venue is a role, not a type: a café, hotel, synagogue or park can be the venue.
- Do not make an event of every season of a long career: one `season` event per stated engagement (troupe + place + period) is enough; repeated performances of one play in one engagement = one event.

F. PERFORMANCE FACTS: {"person": id, "function", "character": verbatim or "", "work": work id, "org": id or "", "venue": id or "", "place": id or "", "date_start", "date_end", "language", "status", "event": event id or "", "in_quote": false, "evidence"}
- function (person -> work): author | translator | adapter | composer | lyricist | librettist | director | designer | choreographer | performer | producer | prompter | conductor | other (+ "function_text" with the word as written)
- "Played Ben Hador in Shulamis" with no troupe, place or year is a complete fact. Never invent a production; leave the optional fields "".
- Authorship of a play the subject WROTE: record it only when the text also says something about it here (staged, translated, adapted, banned, a premiere). A bare list of the subject's works is bibliography: skip, except to record each listed work once in `works`.
- work_relations: {"from": work id, "to": work id, "type": "translation_of"|"adaptation_of"|"based_on"|"same_as", "evidence"} when stated.

G. SOURCES: in `mentions.sources`, one per informant or cited text. Also `source_uses`: {"source": id, "use": "informant"|"quoted"|"corrected_or_contradicted"|"cited", "about": short English note on what it supports, "evidence"}. Typical signals: "לויט ... מיטטיילונג", "דערציילט", "שרייבט", "אין זיינע זכרונות", "אין אַ בריוו צום לעקסיקאָן", the bibliography lines at the end of the entry (each cited article = one cited_text source).

CONTROLLED LISTS
occupation: actor, actress, singer, chorister, dancer, comedian, prompter, director, stage_manager, conductor, composer, lyricist, librettist, playwright, translator, critic, journalist, editor, publisher, teacher, cantor, meshorer, badkhn, folk_singer, manager, lessee, impresario, agent, cashier, administrator, publicist, set_designer, costume_designer, choreographer, photographer, film_director, radio_announcer, other
work_kind: play, operetta, opera, musical_comedy, melodrama, sketch, one_act, revue, song, music, film, radio_play, book, memoir, article, periodical, comedy, tragedy, farce, purim_play, drama, other ("" if the entry does not say)
Dates: "YYYY", "YYYY-MM" or "YYYY-MM-DD"; seasons like "1912-13" -> date_start "1912", date_end "1913".

OUTPUT: ONLY one JSON object:
{"entry_id": ..., "part": ..., "mentions": {"persons": [...], "organizations": [...], "places": [...], "works": [...], "sources": [...]},
