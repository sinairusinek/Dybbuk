How this was made

  • Corpus. The 68 entries of the Leksikon that mention a Dybbuk and a French place or the French language.
  • Extraction. Gemini 3 Flash read each entry for Dybbuk–France facts. Its draft was the starting point only: every fact here was checked against the entry by hand, and about two thirds of the drafted rows were dropped.
  • Evidence. Each fact quotes the entry verbatim and links to its volume, columns and the archive.org scan (vols. 1–6).
  • Tiers. “Played in France” requires the text to place a Dybbuk performance in France. “Tour” means a company carried the Dybbuk in its repertoire on a tour that reached France. “In French” covers translations, reported French stagings and French reviews. “In France” means a Dybbuk person or company was in France without, as far as the Leksikon says, playing it there.
  • See also: The Dybbuks (every work of that name) and the France dossier.
