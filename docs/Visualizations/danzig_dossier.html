Data problems found

Three org-layer problems, each detailed in §4. They are recorded here for review; this dossier changes no data.

  1. The 1921 Moving Theatre is merged into the 1938 Balagan-yidn. Its two clusters (ORG-C02772_Q02, ORG-C01787) were coined as db 727 / 728 under the name באַלאַגעניידען and merged into db 497. The correct row, db 725, exists. Sarna's 1921 engagement therefore points at a 1938 theatre.
  2. A touring troupe is recorded as resident. Jonas Turkow's troupe (org:764) is located_in Danzig on the strength of one 1939 sentence. Every edge on the troupe, back to 1927, inherits the location.
  3. One theatre may be split across four nodes. ORG-C01387_Q24, org:726, the Gdańsk collective and org:733 Kultur-bund-teater all describe the resident Yiddish stage of 1933–38. This is a merge candidate, not a conclusion.

The bio extraction produced one person↔Danzig fact. It is an extraction error:

A theatre was "born" in Danzig. Entry P-7-facs_17_tr_1756390458 is about the kleynkunst theatre Balagan-yidn, not about a person. It says the theatre איז אָרגאַניזירט געוואָרן אין דאַנציג and, later, that it איז געבוירן געוואָרן אין אַן אומגליקלעכן מאָמענט ("was born at an unfortunate moment"). The extractor joined the metaphor to the place. This edge is the "1 person" in the gazetteer count and the only birth attestation in Kimatch. Group entries (troupes, theatres) should be excluded from the bio pass.

Leyze Deykhes's edge points to a real event, stored as a pseudo-place. In 1945 the Germans marched the camp inmates on foot קיין דאַנסק (דאַנציג) to be killed. She was shot trying to escape the death march. The edge belongs on an event (a death march toward Danzig), not on a place called "on the way to Danzig".

After removing the error, the bio layer holds no true Danzig facts. That fits the corpus: in the Leksikon, Danzig is a station (people played, edited, emigrated through, or got stuck there), never a birthplace. The one burial in the text, Melekh Rubin's wife (1930), belongs to someone who has no person node.

One theatre or four? Weinshtok managed the "Yiddish theatre in Danzig" in 1937, the same year Weiner and Karpinovitsh led Turkow's "Kultur-bund-teater". These may be stages of one enterprise under changing management. That is a merge candidate for review, not a conclusion.

5 · What the lexicon narrates: four Danzig layers

Refuge station after the 1883 ban

Four entries (Spivakovsky, Zuckerman, Yosef Weinshtok, Leon Berger) tell the same story. After the Russian ban of 17 August 1883, a troupe of Zuckerman, Spivakovsky, Shayngold, Sore Goldshteyn-Karp, Goldshmidt, Weinshtok and the Kaufmans left Russia under the prompter Berger. They played two months in Königsberg, two weeks in Danzig, then Berlin, billed as an "Oriental operetta company" and performing שולמית in German. Danzig also appears as a trading partner: Peretz's father "traded with Danzig" and floated timber down the Vistula, and Max Davis's family in Mława imported fish from Scandinavia and Danzig.

The Free City and the German-Jewish audience (1916–1932)

Yehuda-Hersh Shayak edited the דאַנציגער לעבען and then the דאַנציגער טאָגבלאַט during his wanderings from 1916 until 1927. In 1921 Turkow and Diana Blumenfeld's Moving Yiddish Dramatic Theatre began its tour in Danzig, with Tsesha Sarna in the female leads. It flopped in the large Kaiser-Wilhelm hall "before empty benches": there was not yet any large East-European immigration, and the German Jews האָבן זיך מיט ביטול באַצויגן צו אַלץ, וואָס ס'האָט געשמעקט מיט 'יידיש'. The troupe could not afford to leave until Sarna's husband sent money. Eli Mintz lived in Danzig in 1924–27 as a waiter and then a haberdasher, acting with the Peretz-farayn amateurs. Katzenelson's 1922 play appeared under the Sfinks imprint Warsaw–Danzig–Łódź. Max Erik worked on Old-Yiddish texts in the Danzig city library. In 1930 Melekh Rubin's emigrant family turned back mid-ocean when his wife died on board. Having given up Polish citizenship, they could land only in the פֿרייִשטאָט דאַנציג. Rubin buried her there and organized Yiddish performances until 1932.

The Ostjuden stage under Nazi pressure (1933–1939)

With a sizeable East-European community now in the city, Rudolf Zaslavsky, his brother Naumov and his wife founded a Yiddish theatre in 1933–34. It played several nights a week in winter, in theatre halls for the hits and in German-style beer halls and club rooms for the rest. From 1936 a run of resident companies follows: the Danziger Yiddish theatre (Neymiler, Dogim), Weinshtok and Hersh Glovinsky's management (1937), and the Kultur-bund-teater, which Turkow took over in 1938 and staffed with Sholem and Clara Zucker and Ester Gruber. Turkow's account, quoted in Zucker's entry, is the most vivid Danzig passage in the corpus. Going to the theatre in "Nazi Danzig" was גיין אויף קידוש השם. Actors arrived each night with black eyes. The audience forced the troupe to stay for three more months, until Nazi thugs stoned the theatre, attacked the departing audience and lay in wait for the actors. Then they left for Warsaw. (Zucker's "great pogrom" is presumably the November 1938 riots in Danzig; that date is our gloss, not the entry's.) In 1939 Turkow staged Katsizne's דוכוס, and Weinshtok was playing with him in Danzig when the war began. Ugier's letter of that summer mentions "a new provocation in Danzig" among the headlines that gave the writer heart palpitations.

Afterword (1945)

Danzig returns once more as the destination of a 1945 death march from the camps around Toruń. Leyze Deykhes was shot escaping it, and the narrator survived in Bydgoszcz.

Two source conflicts. (1) Laura Glicksman's entry quotes Turkow's own sentence as "in 1931, when I played in Danzig with the Moving Theatre". The Moving Theatre entry and Sarna's entry give 1921, the year the troupe was founded, so 1931 is a misprint. (2) Turkow places Balagan-yidn's founding in Danzig. M. Nudelman, in the same entry, describes it as Broderzon's post-Ararat troupe "without a fixed playing place", moving from town to town. Ester Gruber's entry has her joining it after her Danzig season. The KG's located_in follows Turkow alone.
