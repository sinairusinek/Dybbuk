typenwhat it is
person4,983A real person, resolved against people_db. Carries a db id when aligned; an unaligned subject keeps its Lexicon entry id.
play273A play as plays_db records it. The unit an ascription verdict attaches to — see the attribution guard below.
edition27A printed or manuscript witness of a play, joined by title.
production_event4,119An event: a staging (identified by a production_key), or a person's birth, death or burial. The vitals became events in step 9c — an event holds the date, the place and the other people involved, which the born_in / died_in / buried_in edges could not. The 167 edges that survive are the ones where the two sources disagree, held for review.

venue31A building named as a performance site but not resolved to an org row.
place1,376A settlement, resolved against the toponyms gazetteer (Wikidata qid in ext_ref_id).

Node id conventions

patternmeaning
person:<people_db id>a person aligned to people_db
person:entry<id>a Lexicon subject with no people_db row yet
person:ANONan anonymous relative named only relationally (פָאטער…), one per host entry
play:<plays_db id>a play
org:<core_db id>an organisation resolved to core_db
org_cluster:<cluster id>an extraction cluster NOT yet resolved to a core_db row; its review decision is in notes
place:<gazetteer id>a settlement
event:<production_key>a staging

Edge ids are not stable. edge_id renumbers on every build_kg.py run; reference an edge by its endpoints and provenance_fact_ids, never by its id.

Relation to the Lateiner/Hurwitz graph

The project keeps two graphs over overlapping material. They are joined, not merged, and they share no relation names at all — the join is Zylbercweig/plays/kg_crosslink.py, which maps catalogue work ids to plays_db play ids and carries the ascription verdicts and open questions both ways.
