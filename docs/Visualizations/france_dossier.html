Data problems found — and fixed

Five problems surfaced while building this dossier. Four are fixed (2026-11-15). The fifth is a coverage gap, not an error.

  1. Ten Eastern European towns sat on French communes. Grodzisk, Nowo-Minsk, Terespol, Komarno, Szczerzec, Novyi Buh and Rava-Ruska were resolved to Wikidata items for French villages (Gugney-aux-Aulx, Saint-Carné, Sombrin…). Olomouc, Slavuta and two Galician villages had the right QID but a French village's label. Together that put 23 births in Lorraine, Picardy and Brittany. Fixed through a new hand-override table that the gazetteer build applies first (zibn-shtern/data/reference/place_qid_overrides.tsv). The gazetteer and the KG were rebuilt (§1).
  2. Left Poale Zion was "in" a French canton. א'י, the abbreviation for Eretz-Israel, had been matched to the canton of Ay. Fixed: org:2608 is now located_in Land of Israel (Q155321).
  3. Sammy Gronemann was "born in Strasbourg". The entry says שטראָסבורג / מערב-פּרייסן, Strasburg in West Prussia. Fixed: his birth now points to Brodnica (Q690030). The override is scoped to his record only, since the same spelling elsewhere is Strasbourg.
  4. Touring companies were recorded as resident. Fixed for the whole KG: orgs typed Traveling Company or Company on Tour now get toured_in instead of located_in (733 edges). Orgs typed "Not an organization", such as Drancy camp and the Vladek children's home, no longer become org nodes (34 relations dropped) (§4).
  5. The war is nearly absent from the KG. The bio layer records "died in X" only. Deportation, internment and flight never become edges, while the itineraries hold 71 French stations for 1939–45 (§7). This is a gap in what the KG extracts, not a wrong fact. Closing it means adding itinerary edges (stayed_in / toured_in / deported_from) as a KG layer, which three place dossiers now support.

The densest layer. It covers the founding of the Yiddish actors' union (Troyman, 1914) and the Peretz-Theater (1915), then the resident companies: Blumenthal, Burshteyn (the Yiddish Folks-Theater, 1925; the first revue theatre, 1926), Kompaneyets, the theatre in the rue de Lancry, and PIAT under David Licht (from 1934). Metz had a Yiddish community stage after 1919. Guests came constantly: the Vilna Troupe (also Metz, Nancy, Strasbourg), Maurice Schwartz (1924, 1929), Kessler, Zaslavsky, Fishzon, Molly Picon at the Alhambra (1931), the Yiddish Bande (1938), and Modicut's puppets (1929).

9 · Method

  1. Corpus. 391 entries of people/entry_texts.tsv that name Paris, France, Metz, Alsace-Lorraine, Drancy, Vichy, Pithiviers, Gurs, Nice or Marseille (points stripped). 19 of them yielded no French station (the word is a book title, the adjective "French", or similar).
  2. Extraction. itinerary/extract_itineraries.py (Gemini 3 Flash, 6 shards), with a France prompt that adds a country field to every station. 5 entries returned malformed JSON twice and are not covered: P-1-facs_347_TextRegion_1710166960652_499, P-4-facs_625_tr_1755029931, P-1-facs_290_r_1, P-3-facs_158_tr_1755156151, P-4-facs_622_tr_1755030211.
  3. Resolution. Shared resolver (build_itinerary_data.py), then the France-only france_dossier/resolve_france.py for spellings the gazetteer lacks. A station counts as French when Gemini says so or its QID is French.
  4. Sources. Each row links to the Leksikon volume, columns and archive.org scan via lexicon_ref.py (vol. 7 is not online). Review table: plays/france_dossier/france_stations_resolved.tsv.
