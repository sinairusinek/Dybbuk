Extraction schema, v2

What the next pass over all seven volumes of the Lexicon will extract: eight kinds of entity and the relations between them. Solid lines are already in the knowledge graph; dashed lines are new in this pass. Click any entity or relation for its fields and types.

How to read it

Everything is extracted as written: names as they appear, with a short verbatim quote as evidence. Within one entry the extractor resolves פּ., ער and short surnames to the fullest form in that entry. Which person a name refers to across the Lexicon is decided afterwards, in entity resolution, which uses these relations as evidence.

Person → organization relations (18.5k edges, aligned through core_db) are not re-extracted. “X played in Y’s troupe” becomes two such edges — Y leads the troupe, X performed in it — not a person-to-person link.

Where the text rarely decides a distinction (theatre building vs company, hall vs theatre), the schema has no field for it; what the text does state is recorded as dated facts instead.

Anything that does not fit the schema goes to schema_issues, with a question.
